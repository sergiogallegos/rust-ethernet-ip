Industrial safety notice

Communication success is not a safety guarantee. Validate the library, wrapper, controller firmware, route, tag definitions, failure behavior, and reconnect behavior in an isolated test environment before production deployment.

Do not direct automated write, endurance, or performance tests at safety logic, motion, physical outputs, production recipes, or any tag that can move equipment or create a hazardous condition. Use dedicated test tags and follow the site's safety policies, change-control process, and applicable regulations.
