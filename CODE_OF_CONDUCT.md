# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional in discussions.
- Assume good intent and focus on technical clarity.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Prioritize collaboration and safety-minded engineering.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Threatening, abusive, or demeaning language.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or reviews.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project maintainer via GitHub (open an issue and mark it `Code of Conduct`, or contact `@sergiogallegos` directly).

All reports will be reviewed and acted on in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments/issues/PRs that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
