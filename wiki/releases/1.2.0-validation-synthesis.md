# 1.2.0 Validation Synthesis

## Summary

`confirmed`: `1.2.0` shipped on 2026-07-08 after a four-binding physical
hardware gate on CompactLogix `5069-L330ERM` firmware `38`.

## Current Understanding

- Rust, C#, Python, and C/C++ each completed 2,338 reads and 2,319 writes plus
  read-back verification with zero unexpected anomalies.
- The exercised surface includes controller/program scope, atomic values,
  arrays, packed BOOLs, built-in and custom strings, nested UDT members, UDT
  arrays, batch operations, and fragmented large structures.
- Handle-aware STRING writes supersede the earlier assumption that custom
  string UDT members were firmware-blocked.
- This is exact evidence for one model/firmware combination, not a blanket
  certification of all 5069 or ControlLogix processors.
- `1.2.1` preparation should re-run physical hardware because program-scoped
  discovery paging/scope propagation changed after the `1.2.0` tag.
