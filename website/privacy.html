Privacy policy

Effective August 21, 2026. This policy describes the project website as shipped in this repository.

Short version: the site has no accounts, forms, advertising, analytics, cookies, or project-operated tracking. One local preference remembers the selected quick-start language.

Information this site collects

The website code does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not use cookies, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or fingerprinting. The interactive language tabs store only the selected language name (for example, csharp) in your browser's local storage so the site can restore that preference. It stays on your device and is not sent to this project. Mobile navigation runs only in your browser.

Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser details, requested path, and timestamp for delivery, security, and operational logs under that provider's own policy. The project logo and favicon are currently loaded from GitHub; visiting the site therefore sends a normal asset request to GitHub.

External links

Links to GitHub, docs.rs, crates.io, NuGet, PyPI, and other services take you to independent websites. Their privacy practices and cookies are governed by their own policies.

Future changes

If analytics, forms, newsletters, or other data-collecting features are added, this policy and the site will be updated before those features are enabled. Material changes will be recorded in the repository.

Questions

Open a GitHub Discussion for general questions. Do not post private or sensitive information in public issues or discussions.
